# Byzantine, 1062: the astrolabe of Sergios (Brescia)

Drawing: [svg](../drawings/byzantine-sergios-1062-brescia.svg), [png](../drawings/byzantine-sergios-1062-brescia.png)

## The instrument

A brass astrolabe dated 1062, at Brescia. Verses round the rete name its maker: in O. M. Dalton's translation, "Sergius the Persian, holding a consul's rank". Dalton published it in 1926 with plates of the front and of the body, which is itself engraved as a plate.

## What the drawing shows

The front: the rete (the pierced star map) with fourteen star pointers, Greek zodiac names, star names and the verses; under it the plate (tympan) engraved on the body, for latitude 36°; the limb (rim) with Greek numerals; the throne above.

## Read from the photographs

- The rete's layout and widths. Each pointer is a small block with one peak; Vega's is a bird, Arcturus's a crescent.
- The zodiac band's scale: lines every 2° and 6°. Whether the edge ticks are every 1° or 2° the photograph cannot settle; Dalton gives 2°.
- The limb: 0.075 ± 0.010 of the outer radius wide, a one-degree ladder inside and 72 numeral cells outside. Twenty-three numerals were read (5 to 70 and 320 to 360), each naming the end of its cell, clockwise from the throne.
- The plate's latitude, measured on Dalton's Plate II: 36.4° ± 0.6°. Almucantars (circles of equal altitude) every 6°; eleven hour lines; four of the twelve hour numerals. No azimuth circles were detected.
- The throne: a crest with seven piercings, 0.284 ± 0.015 of the limb's outer radius high.

## Continued by rule

- The other 49 limb numerals, in shadow on the plate, follow the run.
- Every pointer's tip stands on its star's computed place for 1062 (obliquity 23.56°), not where the photograph shows it. On the photograph several pointers stop short of their stars, so three of ours are taller than the originals.

## Inferred

- Capella's pointer is not visible and is restored in the plainest form.
- The words ΑΝΑΤΟΛΗ (east) and ΔΥϹΙϹ (west) are Dalton's; which side each stands on is inferred from the lengths of the two labels seen there.
- On the limb, the forms of sigma and koppa could not be read; the lunate sigma, round koppa and the stigma for 6 are our choice.
- A second circle just outside the tropic of Capricorn (1.031 times its radius) is drawn as measured. We cannot explain it.

## Left out

The plate's two-line inscription and its almucantar numerals, which are seen but not legible. One small pointer near the hub that matches none of Dalton's fourteen stars. The rule and the back.
