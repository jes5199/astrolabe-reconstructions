# Toledo, August 1068 (Oxford, inv. 55331)

Drawing: [svg](../drawings/toledo-1068-oxford-55331.svg), [png](../drawings/toledo-1068-oxford-55331.png)

## The instrument

An astrolabe by Ibrahim ibn Sa'id al-Sahli, made in Toledo in August 1068 (Shawwal 460), now in the History of Science Museum, University of Oxford.

## What the drawing shows

The front: the rete (the pierced star map) with all 28 of its pointers over the plate (tympan) for Toledo, latitude 40°, inside the limb (the rim with the degree scale), with a broad lobed throne and the shackle above.

## Read from the photographs

- The rete's frame, with its arches, trefoils and bird, and 28 pointers shaped as curved horns. The museum's catalogue lists no stars for this rete, so the count is taken from the photograph. Every tip stands where the maker put it. Comparing the tips with the stars' places in 1068 identifies 26 pointers, three of them doubtfully; two are unidentified and kept.
- The zodiac band: sign symbols, dividers and a 5° scale as engraved; the names stretched as engraved, tops outwards. Nine star names were read.
- The Gemini cell. The catalogue gives one word for Gemini; the letters on the instrument spell another, "the twins". The drawing follows the instrument.
- The limb: all 72 numerals were read, 5 to 360 clockwise, in the Maghribi (western) order of the abjad letter-numerals, with their tops towards the centre.
- The throne: nine round lobes, plain.
- The plate: latitude 40 as engraved; almucantars (circles of equal altitude) every 5°; azimuth circles every 9°, above the horizon only; eleven unequal-hour lines; two lines for the afternoon prayer; two dotted twilight arcs; and a sixth line that the catalogue does not list for this face.

## Continued by rule

- The rete's ecliptic ring is constructed for the obliquity of 1068, 23.56°. The ring on the photograph is proportionally a little smaller below, which would imply about 24.6°.
- On the plate, the almucantar numbers 10 to 25 down each side follow the row's rule; 30 to 55 were read letter by letter.

## Inferred

- The star identifications.
- The shackle is of the omega type according to the catalogue, but both photographs show it edge-on. It is drawn as photographed, an upright leaf. No ring appears in any photograph, and none is drawn.

## Left out

The rete's knobs, the pin and horse, the plate's lug, the back and the alidade. On the plate: the outer row of almucantar numbers, the names of the two twilight arcs (too small to read which is which) and the name of the sixth line (unread). "East" and "West" stand 2° to 4° further up the horizon and smaller than engraved, to clear the hour letters.
